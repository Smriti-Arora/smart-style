Navy blue: navy blue topwear pairs well with white, beige, khaki, grey and light blue bottoms. Navy trousers or jeans work with white, pink, light blue and grey tops. Brown or tan shoes and white sneakers both suit navy.

Black: black is neutral and goes with almost every colour. Black tops suit blue jeans, grey trousers and white bottoms. Black shoes are the safest choice for formal and party wear.

White: white tops and shirts go with jeans, black, navy, khaki and olive bottoms. White sneakers match casual outfits in every season.

Grey: grey is neutral. Grey tops pair with black, navy, white and denim bottoms. Avoid pairing grey with grey in the same shade; vary the tone.

Blue jeans: blue denim bottomwear suits white, black, grey, red and pastel tshirts and shirts. For casual outfits use sneakers, casual shoes or sandals.

Beige and khaki: beige, khaki and cream bottoms pair with navy, olive, white, maroon and pastel topwear. Brown or tan footwear is the best match.

Red and maroon: red and maroon tops pair with black, white, navy and blue denim. Keep the rest of the outfit neutral so one bright item stands out.

Green and olive: olive and green topwear works with beige, khaki, white, black and denim bottoms. Brown and white footwear suit it.

Pink and pastel: pink, lavender and other pastel tops pair with white, grey, navy and light denim. Neutral or white footwear keeps the outfit soft.

Brown and tan: brown footwear and belts match navy, khaki, olive, beige and blue denim. Do not mix brown shoes with black trousers.

Casual usage: casual outfits use tshirts, casual shirts, jeans, shorts and chinos with sneakers, casual shoes, sandals or flip flops. Keep colours relaxed and comfortable.

Formal usage: formal outfits use shirts, blazers, trousers and skirts in solid colours like white, navy, grey, black and light blue. Wear formal shoes or heels, and avoid sneakers, flip flops and loud graphics.

Smart casual: smart casual mixes a neat shirt or polo with chinos or dark jeans and casual leather shoes or clean sneakers.

Ethnic usage: ethnic outfits use kurtas, kurtis, sherwanis, salwars, churidars, leggings, patiala and dupattas. Pair a kurta with churidar, pyjama or straight pants, and wear sandals, mojaris, juttis or flats.

Wedding and festive: for weddings and festivals choose rich colours like maroon, gold, royal blue, green and cream in ethnic wear. Men can wear a kurta with churidar or pyjama and juttis; women can wear a kurta set or lehenga with heels, flats or sandals.

Party usage: party outfits can use darker colours, shiny fabrics and bold accessories. Black, red, navy and metallic shades work well, with heels or smart shoes.

Sports and gym usage: sports and gym outfits use tshirts, track pants, shorts and tights in breathable fabric. Wear sports shoes or running shoes, never sandals or formal shoes.

Travel and home: for travel pick comfortable tshirts, joggers or jeans with sneakers or sandals. For home use soft tshirts, shorts, lounge pants and flip flops.

Summer season: in summer use light cotton, linen and loose fits in white, pastel and light colours. Shorts, light shirts, sandals, flip flops and breathable sneakers are best.

Winter and fall season: in winter and fall layer with sweatshirts, jackets, sweaters and blazers over shirts or tshirts. Use darker colours like navy, grey, olive, maroon and black, with jeans or trousers and closed shoes or boots.

Spring season: spring suits light layers, pastel colours and light jackets with jeans, chinos and sneakers or casual shoes.

Men topwear to bottomwear: shirts and tshirts go with jeans, chinos, trousers and shorts. Formal shirts need trousers and formal shoes. A kurta goes with pyjama, churidar or jeans.

Women topwear to bottomwear: tops and tshirts go with jeans, skirts, trousers and leggings. Kurtis go with leggings, palazzos, churidar or jeans. Heels or flats suit skirts and trousers; sandals suit casual wear.

Footwear matching: formal shoes for formal wear, sneakers and casual shoes for casual wear, sports shoes for gym, sandals and flip flops for summer and home, heels and flats for women's party and ethnic wear, boots for winter.

Colour balance: build an outfit with one bold colour and two neutrals. Avoid more than three strong colours together, and match the shoe tone with the belt or bag when you can.

Pattern mixing: pair a printed or striped top with a plain bottom. Avoid two different big patterns together.

Fit balance: if the top is loose, keep the bottom slim or straight; if the top is fitted, a relaxed bottom works.

Karwa Chauth: married women traditionally dress in red, maroon, pink or other rich bright colours, most often a saree or lehenga (a heavy kurta set also works). Pair it with gold jewellery, bangles, a bindi and a matching potli or clutch, and comfortable flats or low heels because the day is long.

Saree styling: a heavy or embroidered saree needs a simple clutch and one statement piece of jewellery; a plain saree can take bold jewellery. Match the blouse and clutch to one colour in the saree.

Men's festive wear: a kurta in a rich colour (maroon, mustard, teal, rust, cream with embroidery) with a contrasting Nehru jacket or waistcoat looks festive; add a classic watch and formal shoes or sandals.
